Explicit opt-in (consent)

By providing your mobile phone number to EyeSymmetry Vision Center and affirming that you want to receive text messages—for example by telling our staff at the front desk or by phone at 410-581-0505, completing a written or electronic consent form, or another clear affirmative step—you expressly agree to receive SMS and/or MMS messages from EyeSymmetry Vision Center at 1-866-319-7249 about appointments and clinic operations. Consent to receive SMS is not a condition of receiving healthcare services from EyeSymmetry Vision Center. We do not treat a lone reply of START as consent by itself.

Staff also record verbal or written consent in our practice software (Iris). Outbound texting is blocked for patients marked do-not-contact until consent is recorded.

  • Check-in or “we’re ready for you” style clinic notices
  • Operational notices related to your care or orders (for example, glasses or contact lenses ready for pickup)

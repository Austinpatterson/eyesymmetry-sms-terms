We only text mobile numbers when we have a basis to contact you for this program. In our Iris practice software, outbound texting is blocked for patients marked do-not-contact. Staff may use **Allow contact** only after they have your consent. A text of START alone does not reopen contact.

## How to opt out

Reply **STOP** to any message from us to opt out of SMS. After you opt out, we will not send further texts to that number for this program, except as needed to confirm the opt-out.

You can also ask staff to mark you do-not-contact.
